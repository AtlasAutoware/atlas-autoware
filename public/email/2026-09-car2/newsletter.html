Car 2 is designed.

Hi everyone,

Last time I wrote about the power board we designed for our car. That idea kept growing. For our second car we've now designed all of the main electronics ourselves, plus a 3D-printed body to hold them. Here's what it looks like.

A render from our design files, not a photo. The lidar, camera and Jetson are simple stand-in shapes.

The electronics

Two boards we laid out ourselves

Car 1 is a pile of off-the-shelf modules joined by a lot of wiring. On car 2, most of that wiring lives on two circuit boards that stack on top of each other.

Drive board

The motor controller, a built-in battery pack with its own protection chip, USB-C charging, a soft-start power switch, and power for the steering servo and lidar.

Brain board

A carrier for the NVIDIA Jetson Orin Nano, based on Antmicro's open-source design, with Power over Ethernet for the camera and a second Ethernet port for the lidar.

The stack, pulled apart. The whole kit is printed in carbon-fiber PETG and bolts onto a Traxxas Slash 4x4 chassis.

What's next

First a test print of the carbon-fiber kit to check that it fits the real chassis, then a few last checks on the boards, then ordering parts. I'll send photos once the real thing starts coming together.
